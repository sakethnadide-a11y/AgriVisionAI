FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AgriVisionAI.

Which crops are currently supported?

AgriVisionAI will support over 150 crop classes and hundreds of plant diseases across major Indian crops.

Can I upload images from my mobile phone?

Yes. You can capture a photo directly from your phone or upload an existing image.

Is my farm history saved?

Yes. Every prediction is stored securely in your account for future reference.

Does it support multiple languages?

Yes. AgriVisionAI is designed to support multiple Indian languages.

Does AgriVisionAI work offline?

Image capture works locally, while AI predictions require an internet connection.
